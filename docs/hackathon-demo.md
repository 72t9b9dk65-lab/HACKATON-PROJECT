# Three-minute hackathon demo

Open the donor and staff views on port 3001. Say once that this is a local prototype with sample accounts and a saved directory of real Hundstallet dogs.

1. **Show the connection.** Start on the central garden. Explain that the virtual shelter grows only when the donor's money is assigned to care products. Click a companion to open their real public profile. The virtual selection is illustrative; beneficiary evidence is attached to transactions.
2. **Show the motivation.** Open Donate and preview a larger contribution. New companions and upgraded areas appear faded, with locked-area funding gaps. Close the preview: no balances changed. Record a demo donation and show that it increases pending, not used.
3. **Make the staff workflow concrete.** Open Add receipts or invoices and load a sample document. Review the extracted units and unequal prices, then save and allocate. Open the donor portfolio that actually received a product. Show the exact product contribution moving from pending to used. Visual growth follows cumulative donations; allocation never unlocks companions a second time. Do not force allocation to a presenter account.
4. **Close the loop.** Attach a permitted demonstration photo to a funded product and select two dogs. Its category and transaction are already linked. Open the donor transaction: the photo, purchased item and exact contribution appear together.
5. **Prove the record can be checked.** Press Verify. Show that it compares the current receipt and allocations with the saved fingerprint. Export the proof and compare the original receipt on /verify. If a prepared Sepolia wallet is available, staff can anchor the fingerprint; never call a local hash a blockchain confirmation.
6. **Show accountability.** Correct a demonstration purchase. Pending increases, growth recalculates and the reversed transaction remains in history with its reason.

The staff view is a working surface, not a reporting project: upload, review, allocate, optionally photo and verify. No story calendar, collaborative features or daily publishing chores are required.

## What is demonstrated

- One exact product price and one donor owner for every new purchased unit.
- No premature spending, duplicate debit or silent redistribution.
- Stable virtual companions, a central garden and five upgrade levels per area.
- Honest separation of actual expenditure, pending money and hypothetical growth.
- Photos linked to purchased products and named beneficiary dogs.
- Portable fingerprints, current-receipt comparison and optional real testnet anchoring.

## Evidence and limits

Automated checks cover money conservation, concurrency, idempotency, whole-product assignment, correction history, source precision, growth and proof tampering. Browser QA covers desktop/mobile, receipt and photo workflows, donor verification, smooth movement, animated sleep indicators and reduced motion.

The blockchain flow is implemented and its validation is tested. No real donation, staff production connection, wallet transaction, push or deployment was performed. A chain fingerprint cannot authenticate the truth of care or substitute for accounting review.

## Pilot questions

Measure receipt-to-allocation time, photo attachment time, OCR correction frequency, donor understanding of pending/used, and repeat contributions. Compare against Hundstallet's current workflow; do not promise invented conversion gains.

Before launch, agree cost attribution and donor communications with Hundstallet, then add authenticated roles, authorized payment reconciliation, privacy controls, backups and operational tests.
